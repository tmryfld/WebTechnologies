About

Tom Rayfield is a black and white photographer based in Yorkshire, UK, specialising in architecture, documentary, and landscape photography.

 


 

Tom's work is driven by a minimalist aesthetic. By stripping away the distraction of colour, he looks to isolate the combination of light, shadow, texture, and form.

Whether observing the clean lines of built environments, the overlooked places of daily life, or the geography of the British landscape, he photographs to find stillness in the visual world.

This portfolio is a carefully curated archive of that work.

His accolades include:

  • Black and White Photography Magazine: Feature
  • Docu Magazine: Feature
  • The Telegraph Newspaper: Feature
  • Ilford FP4 International Competition: Winner
